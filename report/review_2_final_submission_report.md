## 1. Executive Summary

Healthcare delivery in modern hospitals relies heavily on shared departmental workstation accounts (such as `radiology_shared`, `er_triage_shared`, `icu_shared_ws`, and `pharmacy_dispenser`) to avoid latency during critical clinical handoffs. However, when sensitive clinical actions are executed—such as dispensing controlled substances, overriding high-risk dosage alerts, changing treatment regimens, or exporting electronic Protected Health Information (ePHI)—generic audit logs record only the shared account moniker. This creates an accountability void that compromises clinical safety, institutional governance, and regulatory auditability.

Naive heuristics, such as attributing transactions to whatever clinician is listed on shift, collapse whenever multiple practitioners are active simultaneously, achieving only **43.90%** attribution accuracy.

## 2. Problem Statement

Modern clinical departments operate under extreme time pressure. Clinicians frequently move between shared workstations in emergency triage, surgical suites, and intensive care units. In traditional hospital IT architectures:
1. **Generic Terminal Credentialing**: Workstations remain logged in under shared service accounts to avoid 60–90 second individual login delays during trauma resuscitation or cardiac arrests.
2. **Accountability Void**: Audit logs generated by PACS, EHR, and pharmacy automated dispensing cabinets record only `account_id="radiology_shared"`. When an adverse drug event or unauthorized record export occurs, internal investigators cannot establish which human clinician interacted with the software.
3. **Personnel Diversity**: The clinical workforce comprises permanent attending physicians, staff nurses, visiting consultants, rotating interns, and outsourced laboratory technicians. Their shifting physical overlap makes manual duty roster reconciliation inaccurate.
4. **Data Stream Realities**: Hospital telemetry is noisy. Badging systems, network DHCP leases, workstation locks, and shift scheduling logs arrive with varying latencies, occasional clock drift, duplicate packets, and out-of-order deliveries.

A robust solution must ingest multi-source telemetry, separate event occurrence from ingestion arrival, reconstruct logical clinical sequences, and produce explainable evidentiary dossiers rather than speculative guesses.

## 5. Existing Baseline

The naive baseline engine (`src/baseline.py`) models standard hospital departmental auditing:
$$\text{Baseline}(e) = \begin{cases} 
\text{ATTRIBUTED}(u_1) & \text{if } |\{u \in \text{Roster} \mid u \text{ on shift at } t_e\}| = 1 \\ 
\text{AMBIGUOUS} & \text{if } |\{u \in \text{Roster} \mid u \text{ on shift at } t_e\}| > 1 \\ 
\text{UNATTRIBUTED} & \text{if } |\{u \in \text{Roster} \mid u \text{ on shift at } t_e\}| = 0 
\end{cases}$$

### Inherent Flaws of the Baseline
- When two or more nurses or radiology residents are concurrently on duty on a shared terminal, the baseline immediately flags the transaction as `AMBIGUOUS`.
- Out-of-order log entries cause attribution to fail silently.
- Delayed shift assignments cause immediate classification as `UNATTRIBUTED`, with no mechanism for retroactive reconciliation.
- Baseline attribution performance on the 205 sensitive actions: **90 attributed (43.90%)**, **115 ambiguous (56.10%)**, **0 unattributed (0.00%)**.

## 6. Proposed Solution

The proposed multi-signal attribution pipeline replaces naive shift counts with a multi-layered correlation architecture:
1. **Intake Boundary**: Ingests raw telemetry, validates payload integrity, generates intake SHA-256 hashes, and rejects duplicate submissions.
2. **Temporal Buffer**: Places incoming events into an event-time priority queue, allowing late-arriving shift authorizations to arrive within a configurable watermark (default 30s) before final adjudication.
3. **State Machine Lifecycles**: Enforces formal temporal state transitions for shift delegations (`CREATED`, `ACTIVE`, `EXPIRED`, `REVOKED`, `CANCELLED`) and workstation sessions (`CREATED`, `ACTIVE`, `IDLE`, `ENDED`, `EXPIRED`, `TERMINATED`).
4. **Cross-Source Correlation Engine**: Deterministically scores candidate users by combining delegation validity (+40 pts), session binding (+30 pts), device fingerprint match (+15 pts), and subnet CIDR match (+10 pts).
5. **Evidentiary Dossier**: Produces a structured explainable record classifying evidence into `STRONG`, `SUPPORTING`, `MISSING`, `CONFLICTING`, or `INSUFFICIENT`.
6. **Escalation & Cryptographic Audit**: Routes ambiguous and conflicting transactions to a human compliance queue while committing all machine decisions and human adjudications to an append-only SHA-256 audit chain.

## 8. Data Sources and Normalization

Implemented in `src/normalization.py` and `src/multi_source_ingestion.py`, the normalization layer unifies five independent synthetic hospital data streams into a canonical 17-field representation:

```python
@dataclass

    event_id: str             # Unique UUID identifier
    source: str               # Log origin (APPLICATION, AUTH, SESSION, DELEGATION, TELEMETRY)

    shared_account: str       # Workstation account (e.g., radiology_shared)
    user_id: Optional[str]    # Direct clinician ID if asserted
    session_id: Optional[str] # Active terminal session token
    action_type: str          # Specific clinical action (e.g., DISPENSE_NARCOTICS)

    device_id: str            # Terminal hardware fingerprint
    user_agent: str           # Client application/browser signature

    delegation_id: Optional   # Active shift delegation reference
    raw_reference: dict       # Unaltered original payload
```

## 9. Delegation and Session Lifecycle

### 9.1 Delegation State Machine (`src/delegation_lifecycle.py`)
- **`CREATED`**: Pre-shift authorization registered; strictly invalid prior to `start_time`.
- **`ACTIVE`**: Current event timestamp falls within $[start\_time, end\_time]$.
- **`EXPIRED`**: Timestamp exceeds $end\_time$; subsequent transactions fail attribution and trigger operational alerts.
- **`REVOKED`**: Rescinded mid-shift by a supervisor; subsequent actions are blocked.
- **`CANCELLED`**: Nullified prior to activation due to shift rescheduling.
- **Historical Invariant**: Historical transactions executed during a previously valid delegation window remain permanently attributable even after the delegation expires or is revoked.

### 9.2 Session State Machine (`src/session_lifecycle.py`)
- **`CREATED`**: Terminal session instantiated upon badge scan.
- **`ACTIVE`**: User actively transacting on the terminal.
- **`IDLE`**: No user interaction for $>15$ minutes.
- **`EXPIRED`**: Inactivity exceeds configurable timeout (default **30 minutes**).
- **`TERMINATED`**: Explicit clinician logout or smartcard withdrawal.
- **Rolling Refresh**: Any authenticated action resets the inactivity timer.

## 10. Attribution and Evidence Model

Implemented in `src/evidence_model.py`, the attribution engine deterministically constructs an `ExplainableEvidenceDossier` for every clinical event. The scoring rubric awards:
- **Valid Delegation Window Match**: $+40$ points
- **Active Session Token Binding**: $+30$ points
- **Department Hardware Device Match**: $+15$ points
- **Subnet CIDR Range Match**: $+10$ points
- **User Role / Department Alignment**: $+5$ points

### Evidentiary Signal Classification
- **`STRONG`**: Direct match on active delegation window and active user roster.
- **`SUPPORTING`**: Subnet CIDR match, device fingerprint match, session activity within idle threshold.
- **`MISSING`**: Network telemetry unrecorded or delayed shift record.
- **`CONFLICTING`**: Overlapping active delegations with identical telemetry.
- **`INSUFFICIENT`**: Clinician identity unknown or absent from hospital personnel directory.

## 11. Delayed / Out-of-Order Event Handling

Implemented in `src/ingestion_buffer.py` to remediate Review #1 Gap A:
1. **Separation of Clocks**: `event_time` (clinical occurrence) is strictly decoupled from `arrival_time` (buffer intake).
2. **Buffer Window**: In-memory priority queue holds incoming events up to `BUFFER_GRACE_PERIOD_SECONDS = 30`.
3. **Retroactive Reconciliation (`reconcile_pending_events`)**:
   - Initial State: Event `E_DELAY_001` arrives with missing delegation $\rightarrow$ Marked `PENDING`.
   - Late Context: Shift delegation arrives 15 minutes later.
   - Execution: Engine re-evaluates the pending record in-place, updating status to `ATTRIBUTED`.
   - **No Duplicate Record**: The primary key and record identity are preserved; zero duplicate rows are created.
   - **Measured Reconciliation Rate**: **15 / 15 delayed events reconciled (100.00%)**.
4. **Out-of-Order Sequencing**: Events arriving out-of-sequence (e.g., Action at 09:20, Login at 09:10, Delegation at 09:15) are sorted by event timestamp prior to evaluation, restoring causal order.

## 12. Duplicate Detection and Audit Chain

### 12.1 Duplicate Intake Filtering
- Intake payloads are hashed using SHA-256: $\text{hash} = \text{SHA256}(account + timestamp + action + resource + session)$.
- Incoming events matching an existing active hash table entry are immediately rejected with status code `DUPLICATE_EVENT_REJECTED`.
- In standard synthetic intake testing (1,050 total events presented, 50 duplicate events injected):
  - Duplicate presentation rate: **4.76%**
  - Boundary rejection efficiency: **50 / 50 (100.00%)**
  - Post-deduplication persistent duplicate rate: **0.00%** (zero duplicate records stored in database).

### 12.2 Tamper-Evident SHA-256 Audit Trail (`src/audit_chain.py`)
- Each audit record is cryptographically linked to its predecessor:
  $$\text{record\_hash}_n = \text{SHA256}(n \parallel \text{timestamp} \parallel \text{actor} \parallel \text{action} \parallel \text{details} \parallel \text{record\_hash}_{n-1})$$
- `verify_chain()` walks the chain from genesis block to tip, verifying hash continuity.
- Validated by unit tests detecting modified records, deleted blocks, and broken previous-hash links with 100% accuracy.

## 24. Ethics, Privacy and Safety

The system is governed by ten mandatory ethical tenets documented in `docs/ethics_and_limitations.md`:
1. **Attribution Is Not Proof of Intent**: Software establishes temporal and session correlation; it does not adjudicate clinical malice, negligence, or legal liability.
2. **Investigative Support Only**: The system functions strictly as an audit tool for compliance officers; it is prohibited from initiating automated employment termination or credential revocation.
3. **No Evidentiary Concealment**: Missing or conflicting signals are explicitly displayed; uncertainty is never hidden to artificially inflate statistics.
4. **Mandatory Human Review**: All ambiguous cases must halt automatic resolution and route to human escalation.
5. **Telemetry Is Supporting Context**: Network IP, subnet CIDR, and browser fingerprints never independently prove identity.
6. **100% Synthetic Data**: All 18 staff members, 6 accounts, 26 delegations, and 364 log events are deterministically synthesized.
7. **Zero Protected Health Information**: No real patient medical records, social security numbers, or clinical histories are stored.
8. **Zero Live Hospital Integration**: Operates locally in memory; zero live network connections to clinical EHRs or PACS.
9. **No Production Compliance Certification**: Results represent laboratory measurements; they do not constitute HIPAA Security Rule or 21 CFR Part 11 certification.
10. **Human Oversight Required**: Any consequential decision affecting a clinician's professional standing mandates independent investigation.

## 26. Limitations

1. **Synthetic Clock Simulation**: Clinical shifts and event sequences are modeled using simulated datetime offsets rather than real-time hardware clock synchronization.
2. **Local Single-Node Architecture**: Priority queues and SQLite databases run on a single host; distributed clustering and consensus protocols (e.g., Raft) are omitted.
3. **Physical Shoulder-Surfing / Relay Vulnerability**: If Clinician A walks away from an active workstation without badge logout and Clinician B executes an action within the 30-minute idle window, software attribution initially assigns the action to Clinician A. Resolving physical piggybacking requires biometric sensors or smartcard badge lanyards outside software scope.
4. **NAT Subnet Granularity**: When multiple workstations operate behind a shared clinical NAT gateway, network corroboration drops to subnet-level precision.
